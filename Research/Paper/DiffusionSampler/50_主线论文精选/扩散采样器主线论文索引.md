## 它们分别解决什么问题？

- [[DDPM]]：建立离散扩散采样范式
- [[Score-Based Generative Modeling through Stochastic Differential Equations]]：统一 reverse SDE 与 probability flow ODE
- [[Denoising Diffusion Implicit Models]]：第一次把采样步数显著降下来
- [[Pseudo Numerical Methods for Diffusion Models on Manifolds]]：把扩散采样显式写成数值方法
- [[Elucidating the Design Space of Diffusion-Based Generative Models]]：把 Euler / Heun / sigma schedule 的工程设计系统化
- [[Fast Sampling of Diffusion Models with Exponential Integrator]]：指数积分器路线
- [[DPM-Solver-A-Fast-ODE-Solver-for-Diffusion-Probabilistic-Model-Sampling-in-Around-10-Steps]]：专门为 diffusion ODE 设计高阶求解器
- [[DPM-Solver++-Fast-Solver-for-Guided-Sampling-of-Diffusion-Probabilistic-Models]]：解决 guided sampling 时的稳定性与速度
- [[UniPC-A-Unified-Predictor-Corrector-Framework-for-Fast-Sampling-of-Diffusion-Models]]：统一 predictor-corrector 并在极少步数下提升质量
