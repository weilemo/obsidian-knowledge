# Syllabus

Source: [MIT OCW 18.06SC Linear Algebra](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-06sc-linear-algebra-fall-2011/)

## Description

This course covers matrix theory and linear algebra, with emphasis on topics useful in physics, economics, social sciences, natural sciences, and engineering. The course parallels the theory-and-application style of Prof. Gilbert Strang's textbook *Introduction to Linear Algebra*.

## Course Format

The OCW version is designed for independent study. It includes:

- Lecture videos by Prof. Gilbert Strang.
- Summary notes for videos, with suggested textbook readings.
- Problem-solving videos for each topic.
- Problem sets with solutions.
- Selected demonstrations.
- Exams with solutions and review materials.

## Main Units

- Unit I: $Ax=b$ and the Four Subspaces.
- Unit II: Least Squares, Determinants and Eigenvalues.
- Unit III: Positive Definite Matrices and Applications.

## Main Text

- Gilbert Strang, *Introduction to Linear Algebra*.
